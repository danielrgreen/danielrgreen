# Daniel Green

I study the geochemistry of fossil teeth to reconstruct ancient environments, seasons and diets related to human and primate origins, and I direct the Harvard–Kenya field program. My research code is kept in private repositories while the work is in progress.

- Miocene environments of the Turkana Basin. Stable isotopes of fossil mammal teeth.
- Seasonality recorded in tooth enamel. Forward and inverse models of enamel isotope profiles.
- Enamel mineralization in primate teeth. Image-analysis pipeline and growth model.
- Primate teeth by ion microprobe. High-resolution isotope analyses with GFZ Potsdam.
- Mammalian ecometrics and past climate. Reconstructing climate from fossil community traits.
- Iguana and Plateosaurus teeth. Isotopes and body-water models in reptile and dinosaur teeth.
- Teaching: Out of the Whirlwind (HEB 196). Course labs on teeth as climate archives.
